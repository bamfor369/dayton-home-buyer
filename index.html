Common questions

Will you pay full market value?

Usually not. Cash offers are typically below full retail value because the house is bought as-is, with no repairs, commissions or financing delays. You decide whether the trade-off is worth it, and there's never any obligation.

Are you a real estate agent?

No. I'm not a licensed real estate broker or agent, and I don't represent you. I'm an investor who buys properties. In some cases I may assign my purchase contract to another buyer for a fee instead of buying the property myself. I'll always tell you this up front, and it's disclosed in writing in our agreement.

Are there any fees?

I don't charge you commissions or fees. How closing costs are split is written into the offer so you know exactly what you'll walk away with before you sign.

Do I have to clean out the house?

No. Leave behind whatever you don't want.

What areas do you buy in?

Dayton and roughly an hour around, including Montgomery, Greene, Miami, Clark, Warren and Butler counties.
